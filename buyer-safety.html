Buyer Safety Tips

⚠️ Important Warning

Do not make full payment before receiving, inspecting, and confirming the product.

✅ Inspect the product first

Check the product carefully before paying. For phones, laptops, vehicles, appliances, and electronics, test everything properly.

🚗 Do vehicle verification

For cars and motorbikes, check the documents, chassis number, ownership details, roadworthy status, and history before payment.

📍 Meet in a safe place

Meet in public places during the day. Avoid hidden locations and do not go alone for high-value products.

🧾 Keep records

Keep chats, receipts, seller phone number, product images, and payment records in case there is a problem later.

🚫 Avoid pressure

Do not rush payment because a seller says many people are interested. Scammers often pressure buyers to pay quickly.
