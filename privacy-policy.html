Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DIDWAPA collects, uses, shares, stores, protects and deletes personal information, including identity-verification photographs and facial data, when people use our online marketplace, website and mobile applications.

1. Introduction

DIDWAPA is an online marketplace operated by DIDWAPA ESCROW LTD ("DIDWAPA", "we", "our" or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly. This policy applies to users of our website, Android application, iOS application and related marketplace services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name and username
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Profile photograph, when provided
  • Gender, if voluntarily provided
  • Date of birth, if voluntarily provided
Optional information: Gender and date of birth are not required to create a DIDWAPA account. Users may leave these fields blank.

Identity Verification Information

When users submit an identity-verification request, DIDWAPA collects:

  • Photograph of the front of the Ghana Card
  • Photograph of the back of the Ghana Card
  • A selfie photograph showing the user's face
  • Information required to assess verification
  • Verification status and related records

Account Information

  • Login credentials
  • Account settings and preferences
  • Account activity
  • Information needed to maintain account security

Marketplace Information

  • Product titles and descriptions
  • Product photographs
  • Prices and categories
  • Listing locations
  • Seller and business information
  • Messages and enquiries made through the platform

Technical Information

Depending on how users access DIDWAPA, we may process device information, browser information, IP addresses, login activity and technical information necessary for security and operation of the platform.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

DIDWAPA allows users to select photographs from their device or capture new photographs when using supported features.

Photographs may be used for:

  • Uploading product images for marketplace listings
  • Adding or updating a profile photograph
  • Uploading business-related images where supported
  • Capturing or uploading Ghana Card and selfie photographs for identity verification

DIDWAPA requests camera or photo library access when users choose a feature that requires it.

For example, a seller may select photographs from their device to create a product listing. A user submitting identity verification may capture a selfie and upload Ghana Card photographs.

Product photographs uploaded to a public listing may be displayed to other users. Ghana Card and selfie verification photographs are not published in marketplace listings.

Camera or photo library access is not required simply to browse marketplace listings.

4. Identity Verification and Facial Data

4.1 Facial Data Collected

DIDWAPA collects a selfie photograph and photographs of the front and back of a user's Ghana Card when the user submits an identity-verification request.

The selfie contains an image of the user's face. The Ghana Card photograph may also contain an image of the user's face and other identifying information.

4.2 Purpose and Use of Facial Data

DIDWAPA uses the submitted selfie and Ghana Card photograph for identity verification. The verification process involves comparing the user's selfie with the photograph appearing on the Ghana Card.

This helps DIDWAPA assess whether the person submitting the verification request matches the identity document provided.

Identity verification is used to support account security, reduce impersonation, identify suspicious verification attempts and help prevent fraudulent activity.

Ghana Card and selfie photographs are not displayed publicly to buyers or sellers.

4.3 Storage of Facial Data

DIDWAPA associates identity-verification photographs with the user's account.

These photographs are stored using the platform's protected hosting and storage infrastructure.

Access to verification photographs is restricted to authorised personnel and systems responsible for identity verification, account security and related administration.

4.4 Sharing of Facial Data

DIDWAPA does not publish or sell users' Ghana Card photographs, selfie photographs or facial-verification information to advertisers or data brokers.

Hosting and infrastructure service providers may process or store verification photographs on DIDWAPA's behalf as necessary to operate the platform.

Verification information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

4.5 Retention of Facial Data

Identity-verification photographs and related verification records are retained while the associated account remains active and the information is needed for verification, account security or related operational purposes.

4.6 Deletion of Facial Data

Users can delete their DIDWAPA accounts using the account-deletion feature.

When an account is deleted, the associated account information, Ghana Card photographs, selfie photographs and related verification records are removed from DIDWAPA's active account systems.

Information subject to legal retention obligations or remaining temporarily in infrastructure backups is handled according to applicable requirements and backup-deletion procedures.

4.7 Privacy and Facial Data Enquiries

Users may contact DIDWAPA regarding identity-verification information, facial-data processing or account deletion at privacy@didwapa.com .

6. Sharing Your Information

DIDWAPA may share information where necessary to operate the platform, provide requested services, maintain security or comply with applicable law.

Depending on the service, recipients may include:

7. Public Marketplace Information

Information included in public listings may be visible to other DIDWAPA users. This can include:

  • Product photographs

  • Seller or business display names

  • Listing locations
  • Contact information deliberately made public

Identity-verification photographs, including Ghana Card and selfie photographs, are not included in public marketplace listings.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in public listings.

8. Data Storage and Security

DIDWAPA uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse and disclosure.

These safeguards may include access controls, secure communications, restricted administrative access and other appropriate security measures.

Identity-verification photographs are associated with the user's account and stored using DIDWAPA's protected hosting and storage infrastructure.

Access to sensitive verification information is restricted to authorised personnel and systems requiring the information for verification and security.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

DIDWAPA retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Identity-verification photographs, including Ghana Card and selfie photographs, and related verification records are retained while the account remains active and the information is needed for verification, account security or related purposes.

When users delete their accounts, the associated verification photographs and records are removed from the active account systems.

Information that must be retained by law or that remains temporarily in infrastructure backups is managed under the applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law. Information temporarily remaining in infrastructure backups is handled under applicable backup-deletion procedures.

Users with account-deletion or privacy questions may contact privacy@didwapa.com .
