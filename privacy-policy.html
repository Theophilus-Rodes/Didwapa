Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DIDWAPA collects, uses, shares, stores and protects information when people use our online marketplace, website and mobile applications.

1. Introduction

DIDWAPA is an online marketplace operated by DIDWAPA ESCROW LTD ("DIDWAPA", "we", "our" or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy applies to users of our website, Android application, iOS application and related marketplace services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name and username
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Profile photograph, when provided
  • Gender, if voluntarily provided
  • Date of birth, if voluntarily provided
Optional information: Gender and date of birth are not required to create a DIDWAPA account. Users may leave these fields blank.

Account Information

  • Login credentials
  • Account settings and preferences
  • Account activity
  • Information needed to maintain account security

Marketplace Information

  • Product titles and descriptions
  • Product photographs
  • Prices and categories
  • Listing locations
  • Seller and business information
  • Messages and enquiries made through the platform

Technical Information

Depending on how you access DIDWAPA, we may process device information, browser information, IP addresses, login activity and technical information necessary for security and operation of the platform.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

DIDWAPA allows users to choose photographs from their device or capture new photographs when using supported features.

Photographs may be used for:

  • Uploading product images for marketplace listings
  • Adding or updating a profile photograph
  • Uploading business-related images where supported

DIDWAPA requests access to the camera or photo library when the user chooses a feature that requires it.

Selected photographs are processed for the feature requested by the user. For example, product photographs uploaded to a listing may be displayed publicly to other users.

Access to the camera or photo library is not required simply to browse marketplace listings.

4. Identity Verification and Facial Data

DIDWAPA may introduce or provide identity verification features for eligible accounts where these features are available.

Identity verification may involve an identification document and a selfie photograph, but such information must only be requested when the applicable verification feature is active and the user has been informed of the purpose.

Current availability: Ghana Card and selfie identity verification are not currently offered through the active mobile application.

If identity verification is enabled in a future version, DIDWAPA will update this policy and provide applicable disclosures about the collection, purpose, recipients, storage, retention and deletion of identification photographs and facial data.

A profile photograph or product photograph is not automatically treated as an identity-verification submission.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Allow users to publish marketplace listings
  • Connect buyers and sellers
  • Provide messaging and customer support
  • Maintain account and platform security
  • Detect suspected fraud and misuse
  • Improve the performance of DIDWAPA
  • Send important service-related communications
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

6. Sharing Your Information

We may share information where necessary to operate DIDWAPA, provide requested services, maintain security or comply with applicable law.

Depending on the service, recipients may include:

  • Technology and hosting service providers
  • Payment service providers where applicable
  • Professional advisers where necessary
  • Government or law enforcement authorities when legally required

Marketplace listings and seller information intended for publication may be visible to other users.

DIDWAPA does not sell users' personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

7. Public Marketplace Information

Information included in public listings may be visible to other DIDWAPA users. This can include:

  • Product photographs
  • Product descriptions and prices
  • Seller or business display names
  • Store information
  • Listing locations
  • Contact information deliberately made public

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in public listings.

8. Data Storage and Security

DIDWAPA uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse and disclosure.

These safeguards may include access controls, secure communications, restricted administrative access and other appropriate security measures.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

DIDWAPA retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Retention may depend on the type of information, account status, security requirements, legal obligations and unresolved disputes.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate and technically feasible.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Update account information
  • Request deletion of eligible information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

Requests can be made through the available account features or by contacting DIDWAPA.

11. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their DIDWAPA account and associated personal information through available account controls or by contacting customer support.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support login sessions, remember preferences, improve performance and maintain security.

Browser settings may allow users to control certain cookies.

13. Children's Privacy

DIDWAPA is not intended for children under 18 to use independently.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.

14. Third-Party Services

DIDWAPA may rely on third-party service providers for hosting, infrastructure and other operational functions.

Our platform may also contain links to external websites whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

15. International Data Processing

Some service providers may process information outside Ghana.

Where applicable, DIDWAPA seeks to use appropriate safeguards consistent with relevant data-protection requirements.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

DIDWAPA may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal obligations or information practices change.

The latest version will be made available through our website or mobile application.
